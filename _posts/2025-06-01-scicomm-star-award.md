Sandip has received a Bronze SciComm Star award from the MRC Toxicology Unit in recognition of his science-communication work, including the *Bio-info* YouTube channel of short scientific explainer videos.
<!--more-->

The channel translates proteomics, ageing biology and bioinformatics concepts into short, visual explanations for students and early-career researchers. New episodes are posted at [@BioInfoChannel](https://www.youtube.com/@BioInfoChannel).
