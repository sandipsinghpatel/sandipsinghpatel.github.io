After four years as a Glenn Postdoctoral Fellow at the Buck Institute for Research on Aging, Sandip has moved to Cambridge to lead the Proteomics Facility of the MRC Toxicology Unit and to start his own research programme on proteomics of healthy brain ageing.
<!--more-->

The facility runs Thermo Orbitrap Exploris 480 and Fusion instruments and supports researchers across the Unit and the University with DDA, DIA, TMT, label-free and targeted proteomics, together with training in sample preparation, quality control and data analysis.
