Requesting data

If a dataset you need is not yet public, or you would like the processed matrices, spectral libraries or analysis scripts behind a particular figure, email skp59@cam.ac.uk and we will share what we can. Clinical datasets are shared within the limits of the consent and ethical approval under which they were collected.
