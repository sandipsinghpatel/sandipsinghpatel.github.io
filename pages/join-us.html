We welcome curious, careful scientists who want to work at the interface of mass spectrometry, cell biology and ageing. You do not need to arrive as a proteomics expert — we train every member on instrument operation, quality control and data analysis — but you do need to enjoy quantitative thinking and collaborative work.

Current opportunities

A.

Postdoctoral researchers

We are happy to support fellowship applications (for example Marie Skłodowska-Curie, EMBO, HFSP, Wellcome Early Career, Newton International, BBSRC/MRC schemes) from candidates with a background in proteomics, neuroscience, cell biology or computational biology. Please write well before the deadline.

B.

PhD students

Prospective students apply through the University of Cambridge and the MRC Toxicology Unit's PhD programme. Projects combining proteomics with iPSC-derived neural models or extracellular-vesicle biology are available; get in touch to discuss ideas.

C.

Visiting scientists and interns

Short visits (three to twelve months) for researchers who would like to learn DIA proteomics, sample preparation or data analysis are welcome, subject to space and funding. Master's and undergraduate placements are considered on a case-by-case basis.

Collaborate with the Proteomics Facility

If you are a Cambridge or external researcher with a proteomics question, the facility can advise on feasibility, experimental design, minimum sample quantities, quantification strategy, approximate cost and turnaround time. Email proteomics@mrc-tox.cam.ac.uk with a short description of your samples and aims.

How to apply

Send a single PDF containing a CV, a one-page statement of research interests explaining why our lab, and the names of two referees to skp59@cam.ac.uk. Please put “Application — [position]” in the subject line.
