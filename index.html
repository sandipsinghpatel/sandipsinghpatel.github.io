Quantitative proteomics to understand how cellular senescence, inflammation and extracellular vesicles shape the ageing brain — and how to intervene.

Ageing across biological length scales

We are a mass-spectrometry proteomics group led by Dr Sandip Kumar Patel at the MRC Toxicology Unit, University of Cambridge. We integrate deep quantitative proteomics with human iPSC-derived neural models and extracellular-vesicle biology to ask how senescent cells and their secretome drive neuroinflammation and neurodegeneration, and how those signals can be measured in blood and targeted therapeutically.

Alongside our own research, Sandip directs the Unit's Proteomics Facility, which provides DDA, DIA, TMT and targeted LC-MS/MS workflows, reproducible quality control, data analysis and hands-on training for researchers across Cambridge and beyond.
